## 🏢 Company Background

**AlexBank** was founded in **1957** and privatized in **2006** after being acquired by Intesa Sanpaolo. The bank operates across Egypt with branches in Alexandria, Cairo, Delta, Giza, and Upper Egypt regions.

### Why Digital Transformation?
- Declining branch visits as customers shifted to online channels
- Fintech competition pushing the bank to modernize
- Legacy systems causing delays and inefficiencies
- Growing demand for mobile and digital banking services

### Transformation Goals
1. **Boost Customer Digital Engagement** — promote mobile and online banking across all age groups
2. **Improve Risk Visibility** — use data and analytics to proactively manage financial risk and defaults
3. **Automate Back-Office Processes** — reduce delays and operating costs for tasks like loan approvals

## 📊 Dashboards

### 1. 👤 Customers Dashboard

**Key Metrics:**
- 1,000 total customers | 247.70M total balance
- Age group 26–35 holds the highest total balance (~75M EGP)
- Savings accounts are the most popular account type (275+ customers)
- Customer count peaked in 2023 after a dip in 2021–2022
- Digital users are concentrated in the 26–35 and 36–45 age groups

---

### 2. 📱 Digital Platform Dashboard


**Key Metrics:**
- Average App Logins: **24.26**
- Basic tier has the highest feature usage, followed by Intermediate and Advanced
- Digital platform activity peaked in **January** (~200 users) and dropped sharply in June
- **Power Users** identified (top logins: CUST000987 ~55 logins)
- **Risk Users** flagged for unusually high login frequency (potential fraud signals)

---

### 3. 💳 Transactions Dashboard


**Key Metrics:**
- 1,000 total transactions | Average transaction: **25.55K EGP**
- Transaction types are nearly evenly distributed: Withdrawal (26.3%), Transfer (25.7%), Payment (25.1%), Deposit (22.9%)
- **Cairo Downtown** branch leads in transaction volume across all types
- Transaction amounts peaked in **Q2** and declined toward Q4
- Mobile and ATM channels are the dominant transaction methods

---

### 4. 💰 Loans Dashboard


**Key Metrics:**
- 1,000 total loans | Top 10 high-risk loans total: **19.92M EGP**
- Personal loans dominate at **40.1%**, followed by Auto (20.1%) and Mortgage (18.5%)
- Delinquency trends peak in **Q2** across loan types
- High default rates observed in **Auto** and **SME** loans
- Strategic response: implement AI-based credit scoring and SME financial planning

---

### 5. 📈 Final Combined Dashboard


**Consolidated view** combining customer engagement, transaction insights, and feature tier usage filtered by region. Key highlights:
- Average Transaction Amount: **24.70K EGP** | App Logins Count: **155**
- 36–45 age group has the highest total balance
- Cairo Downtown branch dominates across all transaction types
- Payment transactions decline steadily from Q2 to Q4
